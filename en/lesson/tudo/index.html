Step by step

Step 1 · SummaryA request’s journey

A customer opens loja.com on her phone. The request reaches the Ingress, the public front desk, which reads the address and forwards it to the api Service.

The Service picks one of the ready pods, on any node, and the answer travels back the same way. All of this in a few milliseconds.

Step 2 · SummaryMeanwhile, behind the scenes

The control plane isn’t part of the request, but it watches everything. The HPA tracks CPU, the controllers count replicas, the scheduler places new pods, and each node’s kubelet runs health checks.

If something goes wrong (a pod dies, a node goes down, traffic doubles), someone on the team notices and fixes it, without waking you up.

Key idea: Your job changes from doing to declaring. You describe the desired state; Kubernetes works nonstop to make reality match.
